# Meeting to actions

Fictional example for the Skilltree framework.

Read the notes for explicit decisions and commitments. Preserve the difference between a suggestion, a decision, and an assigned action.

## Extraction decisions

Keep each action specific enough to complete. Use the owner and deadline exactly as stated. Mark missing ownership or timing as unresolved rather than assigning them yourself.

## Deliverable

Provide a concise decision record, action list, and open questions. Link back to a note or timestamp when available. Drafting the recap does not authorize sending messages, creating tickets, or adding calendar events.
