# Social caption

Fictional example for the Skilltree framework.

Identify the channel, audience, and one useful message in the supplied material. Carry over only claims the source supports.

## Drafting choices

Lead with the specific event or observation. Keep the requested voice and length. Use a link only when its destination is known, and make the call to action match what a reader will find there.

## Deliverable

Offer a few distinct openings with a finished caption for the strongest one. Explain any difference in emphasis briefly. Avoid invented testimonials, engagement figures, or availability claims. Treat every caption as a draft until publication is requested.
