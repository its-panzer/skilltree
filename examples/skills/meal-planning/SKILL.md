# Meal plan

Fictional example for the Skilltree framework.

Use the provided preferences, restrictions, number of people, and available cooking time. Ask about any missing restriction that would materially affect the meals.

## Planning decisions

Reuse perishable ingredients across meals and account for leftovers. Distinguish pantry items from ingredients that need buying. Keep preparation steps realistic for the time available.

## Deliverable

Provide a meal sequence and a grouped shopping list with practical quantities. Label substitutions clearly. Do not invent nutritional targets or treat ordinary meal planning as medical advice.
