# Research a topic

Fictional example for the Skilltree framework.

Define the question and the decision the answer should support. Prefer original documents, datasets, and statements over summaries when available.

## Evidence decisions

Record each source's date and the claim it supports. Compare conflicting evidence at the level of methods and scope. Label your own inference and state what would change the conclusion.

## Deliverable

Lead with the supported answer, then provide the key evidence and remaining uncertainty. Cite claims near the relevant text. Do not imply that an inaccessible source was read or that an absence of evidence proves a claim false.
