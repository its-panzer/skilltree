# Learning sprint

Fictional example for the Skilltree framework.

Identify what the learner wants to do, what they can already do, and the time available. Define a small demonstration that would show progress.

## Practice decisions

Alternate focused practice with feedback on an actual attempt. Choose resources that support the next exercise rather than an exhaustive reading list. Adjust difficulty when the learner's work reveals a specific gap.

## Deliverable

Provide a short sequence of practice sessions, a concrete output for each, and a final check against the original goal. Avoid promising mastery on a fixed schedule.
