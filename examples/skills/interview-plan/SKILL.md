# Interview plan

Fictional example for the Skilltree framework.

State the assumption being tested and who can provide relevant firsthand experience. Keep the session focused on that decision.

## Question design

Ask for a recent concrete example before asking for opinions. Separate observed behavior from intended future behavior. Use neutral follow-ups about context, sequence, and consequences rather than pitching a solution.

## Deliverable

Provide an opening, a small set of core questions, optional probes, and a note-taking structure. Identify what evidence would weaken the initial assumption. Mark invented sample answers as fictional; never present them as participant findings.
