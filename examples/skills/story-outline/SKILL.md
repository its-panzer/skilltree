# Story outline

Fictional example for the Skilltree framework.

Use the supplied premise, genre, audience, and length. When a detail is open, choose a direction and identify it as a creative choice.

## Shape the story

Give the protagonist a visible goal and a reason it matters. Build obstacles that force decisions rather than a sequence of unrelated events. Make the ending follow from the protagonist's choices.

## Deliverable

Provide the premise, a compact cast, and a sequence of scenes. For each scene, state what changes and why the next scene follows. Preserve requested characters and constraints. Do not turn an outline request into a full draft.
