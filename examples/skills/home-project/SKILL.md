# Home project

Fictional example for the Skilltree framework.

Define the desired result, what already exists, and the available time and tools. Work from supplied measurements or inspected conditions rather than guessing critical dimensions.

## Planning decisions

Separate preparation, reversible steps, and work that needs a specialist or further inspection. Keep material quantities tied to measurements. Stop the plan at missing information that could cause damage or make the result unsafe.

## Deliverable

Return a materials list, ordered steps, and a way to check completion. Mark estimates and optional improvements. Do not expand a minor repair into an unrelated renovation.
