# Incident review

Fictional example for the Skilltree framework.

Use the incident record to establish impact, detection, mitigation, and recovery. Preserve uncertainty where the timeline is incomplete.

## Analysis decisions

Separate the triggering event from conditions that allowed the impact to spread. Describe system behavior and available information rather than assigning blame. Avoid labeling a cause as definitive without evidence.

## Deliverable

Provide a concise timeline, contributing factors, and follow-ups tied to a failure mode. Each proposed action should have a verification method; leave owner and deadline open unless assigned in the record.
