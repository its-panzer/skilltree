# Compare options

Fictional example for the Skilltree framework.

Identify the decision, required constraints, and options already under consideration. Ask only for missing criteria that could change the recommendation.

## Comparison decisions

Use the same criteria and units for every option. Verify facts that may have changed, such as pricing or availability. Mark unknowns instead of silently treating them as failures. Explain how the recommendation changes when a major assumption changes.

## Deliverable

Provide a compact comparison and a recommendation tied to the user's priorities. Include a plausible alternative and the condition under which it wins. Do not expand the comparison into an exhaustive market survey unless requested.
